**[ANSWER]**

The correct answer is Option C.

A neural network is a computational model loosely inspired by biological neurons. It consists of layers — an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. Each layer applies mathematical transformations to data and passes results to the next layer.

During training, the network adjusts the strength of connections — called weights — to minimize errors. After enough training data, the network can recognize patterns: images, speech, language, and much more.

All Large Language Models, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, are built on a specific type of neural network called a Transformer — introduced by Vaswani et al. in 2017.
