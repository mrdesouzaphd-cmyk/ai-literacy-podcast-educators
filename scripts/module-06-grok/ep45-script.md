**[QUESTION]**

Question forty-five.

What is a distinctive feature of Grok compared to most other commercial LLMs at its launch?

---

**[ALTERNATIVES]**

Option A — Grok can only process text in English and no other language.

Option B — Grok has real-time access to posts and data on the X platform, giving it up-to-date information from social media as part of its training and retrieval pipeline.

Option C — Grok was the first LLM to score perfectly on all academic benchmarks.

Option D — Grok was designed exclusively for use by professional programmers and does not support general conversation.

---

**[PAUSE CUE]**

Pause and think.

... three ... two ... one ...

---

**[ANSWER]**

The correct answer is Option B.

Grok, developed by xAI, has integration with the X platform — formerly Twitter. This gives Grok access to real-time social media data, which is a distinguishing capability compared to models that rely solely on static training data with a fixed knowledge cutoff.

Grok was also positioned with a more permissive and "anti-woke" alignment stance by its developers, reflecting different design philosophy choices compared to Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's ChatGPT.

For educators, Grok raises important discussions about how the *ownership* and *values* of AI companies influence the behavior and outputs of their models — a media literacy and AI ethics topic.
